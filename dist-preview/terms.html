IN SHORT

Jeevanto is a personal assistant. It helps you keep track of what you've promised, what's coming up and what needs doing, and does things for you when you say go.
You must be 18 or over to use it.
Your account, and everything in it, is yours. We don't sell it, and we never train AI on it.
Jeevanto never pretends to be you. When it books, orders, sends or fills something in, it does it as your assistant, and only after you say go.
Jeevanto can make mistakes. Check anything important before you rely on it.
Express is free. Plus and Family are paid. You can cancel any time, in two clicks.
If something goes wrong, write to us. A person reads every message.

The full terms follow. If anything below seems to say something different from this summary, the full terms apply.

8  Your Circle: Family and kin

8.1  Everyone keeps their own Jeevanto. Being on the same Family plan, or being someone's kin, doesn't give anyone a way into another person's account.

8.2  Nothing is shared without a yes. Each person chooses what to share, and with whom, and can take it back at any time.

8.3  Kin is someone you look after, or who looks after you: a parent, a child, a friend. A care-giver can help only with what the care-receiver has allowed. A care-giver can never:

  • pay with the care-receiver's money;
  • see their passwords;
  • change their settings;
  • send a message as them.

8.4  Notes shared with a care-giver. The care-receiver chooses what to share: one note, certain notes, certain types of note, or all notes. A care-giver can change a note shared with them, but can never delete it. The owner is told of every change, and both of you can see it in your activity. Every note always shows who owns it, and who changed it and when.

8.5  Hand-overs. Between two people on paid plans, one can prepare a booking or a job and hand it to the other. The care-receiver says go, and then either of them may pay. The Plans page shows what can be handed over on each plan.

8.6  Shared store logins can be shared only inside a Family. They can never be bank or email logins, and the password is never shown to anyone.

8.7  Leaving. You can leave a Family, or end a kin link, at any time. You keep everything you made. The other person is told.

9.1  Express is free, for as long as you like. Plus and Family are paid. Prices are on the Plans page, include tax, and are set for your country.

9.2  Free months. Your first 3 months of Plus are free, once per person. They start the first time you open the app. Joining again, or joining a Family, never restarts or extends them.

9.3  Founding price. The first 1,000 people to choose Plus, or each seat of a founding Family, keep the founding price for their first 2 years.

9.4  Paying. On the website, you pay through Razorpay, with UPI Autopay or a card. You set up a standing instruction. Everyone is billed on the 1st of the month: your first payment covers the rest of the month you start in, then your plan renews by itself on the 1st of each month (or once a year, for yearly Plus). Nothing is charged before your free months end. We remind you before each payment. Your card or UPI details stay with Razorpay; we never see them.

9.5  We don't sell plans inside the app. You choose and pay for a plan on jeevanto.com. If we ever offer payment inside the iPhone or Android app, Apple's or Google's own rules will apply to those payments, and we'll update these terms first.

9.6  Cancelling. You can cancel any time, in two clicks, from Your account. Cancelling stops the next payment. Your plan carries on to the end of the period you've paid for. Then you're on Express, with everything you've made kept.

9.7  Refunds are set out in our Refunds and cancellation policy. It forms part of these terms.

9.8  Price changes. If we change the price of your plan, we'll tell you at least 30 days before your next payment at the new price. You can cancel before then. A founding price doesn't change during its 2 years.
