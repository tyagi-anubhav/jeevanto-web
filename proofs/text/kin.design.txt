KIN MODE
Look after the people you love, or let them look after you.
Care that both of you agree to, never one watching the other. Help Papa in his own Jeevanto, or let your daughter help in yours.
His name stays at the top of your screen, so you always know you’re in Papa’s Jeevanto.
When you look after Papa
IN PAPA’S JEEVANTO, AS FAR AS HE ALLOWS
Reminders
Set his medicine reminders from the prescription. His phone reminds him, not yours.
Notes
Keep notes for him, so the details of his care are in one place.
Getting ready
Get him ready for Thursday’s check-up: where, when, what to carry, and where each piece came from.
Finding
If Papa’s plan includes it, you can find what he’s kept, provided he has allowed you to view it.
Lookouts
If Papa’s plan includes it, you can also keep a lookout for him.
His forms
If Papa’s plan includes it, you can fill in his forms inside his Jeevanto, only with his yes. A form that ends in a payment comes back to him to look over; once he says go, he pays, or hands it to you to pay if you’re on Plus or Family. One without a payment goes only if he has allowed you to send it. On your own Plus or Family plan, you can also fill in a form for him from your own Jeevanto, using what he’s shared with you.
Within his plan
What you do inside his Jeevanto depends on his plan, not yours, and uses his allowance. What you do from your own Jeevanto uses yours.
FROM YOUR OWN JEEVANTO
Booking and ordering
From your own Jeevanto, on a paid plan, you can book and order for Papa. If his plan is paid too, hand him a booking or a job to approve, and then either of you pays. If he’s on Express, then you can book, order or get it done yourself, from your Jeevanto.
Always visible
He sees everything you have done for him.
When Tara, your daughter, looks after you
Only what you allow
Tara helps in your Jeevanto with only the permissions you give her.
What she may do
Keep notes and set your reminders, and get you ready for a meeting. If you’re on Plus or Family, she can also find what you’ve kept, keep a lookout for you, and fill in your forms or get a booking ready for you to look over and say go.
What she may see
Nothing you haven’t said yes to.
Never allowed
As kin, Tara can never pay from your account, see your passwords, change your settings or send a message as you. Anything in your account that needs paying comes back to you to look over; once you say go, you pay, or hand it to her to pay if she’s on Plus or Family.
Always visible
You see everything she’s done, and you can pause her help any time.
Kin doesn’t have to be family. A trusted friend nearby can look in on Papa when you can’t.
Tara filled in your passport renewal. Look it over and say go.
Always with a yes
It starts only when both of you say yes: one sends the invitation, the other enters the code.
Every permission is a yes you gave, and every yes can be taken back.
Either of you can pause the help or end the link at any time, and the other is told.
Nothing either of you made is ever lost.
Be present, even when you can’t be there.
Bring the whole family onto one plan,
or add a kin seat for the one you look after.
