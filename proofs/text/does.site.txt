WHAT JEEVANTO CAN DO
Jeevanto is the control system for your commitments, relationships, goals, and decisions.
Each part of Jeevanto is built for one kind of job. Tell it what you need in your own words; it knows which one to reach for.
It remembers, so you don’t have to
Notes
Tell it once. It remembers — and remembers where it heard it.
Reminders
Hand it the timings, and let it hold them — for you, or for Papa.
Prepare
Walk into the meeting ready — with where every point came from.
Find
Ask for anything you’ve kept, and get it back with where it came from.
It gets things done, with your yes
Book
Books the flight, the train or the table — and waits for your yes.
Order
Fills the cart at the shop you use — and remembers what you’re owed.
Do this
Fills in the form for you, and stops for your yes before anything is sent.
Send
Say it the way you feel it. It sends it the way you mean it.
It keeps watch, and sees things through
Lookout
Keeps an eye on what you’re waiting for, and tells you when it changes.
Errands
Hand it a whole errand. It works through it step by step, with your yes at each one.
