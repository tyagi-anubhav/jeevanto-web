OUR PROMISE TO YOU
We never train AI on your data, and we never sell it.
1
WHAT JEEVANTO READS
Only the accounts you choose to connect, what you tell it, and what people in your Circle choose to share with you. That’s how it knows what needs doing. If you connect Google, exactly what Jeevanto does with your Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Contacts is in our privacy policy, section 14.
2
WHAT IT KEEPS
What matters from it, like a date, a promise or an offer, each pointing to where it came from. Never the mail itself.
3
WHAT IT SHOWS YOU
How much it read from each source, each day. Pause any source whenever you like.
4
WHAT TRAINS ON IT
Nothing. We only use AI services set up so your data never trains their models.
5
WHAT IT LEARNS ABOUT YOU
It connects the dots. That’s how it helps. What it learns stays in your Jeevanto, shows how it got there, and can be taken back. A pattern it notices in your habits waits for your yes before it’s used.
6
WHAT YOU CAN TAKE BACK
Anything it knows or has learnt about you. Tell it to forget, and it’s gone.
7
WHO ELSE SEES IT
No one, unless you say yes. Not your family, not the one who looks after you.
8
IF YOU LEAVE
Delete your account any time. Everything goes, and copies in our backups are gone within 7 days. We keep only what the law or fairness needs: a scrambled fingerprint of your email or mobile number, so the free months are given once per person; invoices, for as long as tax law asks; and a record of sign-ins for a year, never what anything said.
9
THIS WEBSITE
No trackers. No ads. One cookie, only if you sign in.
Everything it knows comes from you, or your Circle.
Every fact has a source.
Every action has your hand on it.
Anything it knows, you can take back.
This is what we believe. The privacy policy says the same, in legal terms.
