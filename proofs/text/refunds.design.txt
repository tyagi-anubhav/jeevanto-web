1 What this policy covers
This policy covers payments for Jeevanto's plans made on jeevanto.com through Razorpay. It forms part of our Terms of use.
It doesn't cover things you book, order or pay for on another company's website through Jeevanto. That company's own cancellation and refund rules apply to those (Terms of use, section 6).
2 The plans
2.1 Express is free, for as long as you like. No card, ever.
2.2 Plus and Family are paid. Plus is monthly or yearly. Family is monthly.
2.3 Family has a price per person that falls as the family grows. Every seat pays the same.
2.4 Founding price. The first 1,000 people to choose Plus keep the founding price for their first 2 years. On a Family plan, each seat takes one founding place.
2.5 Prices include tax. They're shown on the Plans page for your country. The price you pay is set by the country of your payment.
3 Your free months
3.1 Plus is free for your first 3 months. Once per person: they're never given twice to the same email address, mobile number or sign-in account, even on a new account.
3.2 They start the first time you open the app, not when you sign up on the website.
3.3 Nothing is charged when they end unless you've chosen Plus or Family. We tell you before they end.
3.4 Subscribing early never shortens them. If you pay during your free months, your paid months start the day after your free months end.
3.5 Joining a Family during your free months doesn't restart or extend them. You keep what's left, and the Family pays for your seat from the day they end.
3.6 A person added to a Family gets the free months only if they've never had them. Their seat isn't charged until those months end. Someone who has already had them is charged from the day they join.
4 How you pay
4.1 You pay on the website, on Razorpay's secure page, with UPI Autopay or a card. Your card or UPI details stay with Razorpay; we never see them.
4.2 When you choose Plus or Family, you set up a standing instruction (an e-mandate) with your bank or UPI app. You pay the first payment that day. After that, your plan renews by itself on the same date each month, or each year for yearly Plus.
4.3 We remind you before every automatic payment: at least 24 hours before, with the amount and the date. You'll also get a receipt after each one.
4.4 You can stop the standing instruction at any time, in your bank or UPI app, as well as by cancelling with us.
4.5 Invoices, showing the tax, are in Your account on the website.
5 Cancelling
5.1 Cancel any time, in two clicks, from Your account on the website. We never make cancelling harder than subscribing, and there's no charge for it.
5.2 Cancelling stops your next payment. Your plan carries on to the end of the period you've paid for. Then you're on Express.
5.3 Everything you've made stays yours. Your notes, reminders, documents and history are all kept. Anything that needs a paid plan waits until you choose one again.
5.4 Moving from Family to Plus takes effect at your next renewal. Everyone keeps Family until the paid month ends. The others on the plan are told the Family plan is ending, never why, and each can take their own plan with nothing lost.
5.5 If a Family plan ends and nobody takes a plan, everyone on it moves to Express, with nothing lost.
6 Refunds
6.1 Monthly plans: no refund for a month already started. The plan runs to the end of that month.
6.2 Yearly Plus, if you leave early (cancel, or move to a Family):
the months you've used are charged at the monthly price. The month you're in counts as used;
whatever is left of your yearly payment is refunded. If you're moving to your own Family plan, it becomes credit on that plan instead;
the yearly price is lower because it rewards staying the whole year, so leaving early doesn't keep that saving.
Example, with sample prices (not our prices): you pay ₹3,499 for a year. You cancel in your fourth month. Four months at ₹349 is ₹1,396, so you get ₹2,103 back. If you cancel in your tenth month, you get about ₹9 back.
6.3 Paid, but never started. If you subscribe on the website and nobody on your plan opens the app within 3 months, we refund your first payment in full and cancel the plan. We remind you after a week and after a month while we hold your place. We never keep money for something nobody used.
6.4 Our mistake. If we charge you twice, charge the wrong amount, or charge you after you cancelled, we refund the difference in full, as soon as we find it or you tell us.
6.5 If we close your account for a reason that isn't your fault, we refund the unused part of what you've paid.
6.6 Apart from these, payments aren't refunded, except where the law says otherwise. Nothing in this policy takes away your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
7 Family plans: adding, removing and leaving
7.1 Adding a seat takes effect at once. Every seat moves to the new family price that day. You pay only the difference for the days left in your billing month, then the new price from your next payment.
7.2 Removing a seat takes effect at your next renewal. The seat stays usable until then. Nothing is refunded for the rest of that month.
7.3 When someone leaves your Family, they keep everything they made and move to Express, or to their own Plus. Their seat stays on your plan, empty, and stays paid until you remove it. We tell you they've left.
7.4 Founding seats. When a founding Family removes a seat, its founding place goes back to the pool when the removal takes effect. A seat added later gets the founding price only if a founding place is free and the offer is still open; otherwise it pays the regular price.
7.5 A Family with both kinds of seat. Founding seats pay the flat founding price, with no family price on top. The family price counts only the regular-price seats: one regular seat pays the full price; two or more move down the family price steps together.
8 Kin seats
8.1 Express includes 1 kin seat. Plus and Family include 2, for the whole plan.
8.2 On Plus or Family you can add more, at one flat price per seat per month. An added kin seat works at once. You pay only for the days left in your billing month, then the full price from your next payment.
8.3 Removing a kin seat works like removing a Family seat (7.2).
9 When a payment fails
9.1 If an automatic payment fails, we tell you straight away, and try again. Your plan carries on for 7 more days while you put it right.
9.2 If it still hasn't gone through after 7 days, the plan ends and everyone on it moves to Express, with nothing lost. On a Family plan, the others are told the plan has ended, never why.
10 When we change or stop something
10.1 Price changes. We tell you at least 30 days before your next payment at a new price. You can cancel before then. A founding price doesn't change during its 2 years.
10.2 If we remove a feature that's part of your paid plan, we tell you in advance. If you cancel because of it, we refund the unused part of the period you've paid for.
10.3 If we stop Jeevanto altogether, we give you at least 60 days' notice, time to take a copy of your data, and a refund of any unused paid period.
11 How and when refunds reach you
11.1 Refunds go back to the way you paid: your card, or the bank account behind your UPI.
11.2 We start a refund within 2 working days of it becoming due. Razorpay then sends it on. Your bank usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it; that part is outside our control.
11.3 We email you when we start a refund, with the amount and a reference.
12 Payments through Apple or Google
We don't sell plans inside the iPhone or Android app. If we ever do, Apple's or Google's own rules will decide refunds for those payments, and we'll update this policy first.
13 Questions and complaints
Help with a payment or a refund: help@jeevanto.com. A person reads every message, and replies within 2 working days.
Complaints: our Grievance Officer, Anubhav Tyagi, at privacy@jeevanto.com. You'll hear from us within 24 hours, and a person will reply within 2 working days. If something needs putting right, we'll do it within 15 days at the latest.
