OUR PROMISE TO YOU
Nothing trains on your data, and it’s never sold.
1
WHAT JEEVANTO READS
Only the accounts you choose to connect, what you tell it, and what people in your Circle choose to share with you. That’s how it knows what needs doing.
2
WHAT IT KEEPS
What matters from it, like a date, a promise or an offer, each pointing to where it came from. Never the mail itself.
3
WHAT IT SHOWS YOU
How much it read from each source, each day. Pause any source whenever you like.
4
WHAT TRAINS ON IT
Nothing. We only use AI services set up so your data never trains their models.
5
WHAT IT LEARNS ABOUT YOU
It connects the dots. That’s how it helps. What it learns stays in your Jeevanto, shows how it got there, and can be taken back. A pattern it notices in your habits waits for your yes before it’s used.
6
WHAT YOU CAN TAKE BACK
Anything it knows or has learnt about you. Tell it to forget, and it’s gone.
7
WHO ELSE SEES IT
No one, unless you say yes. Not your family, not the one who looks after you.
8
IF YOU LEAVE
Delete your account any time. Everything goes, and copies in our backups are gone within 7 days. The only thing we keep is a scrambled fingerprint of your email or mobile number, so offers like the free months are given once per person.
9
THIS WEBSITE
No trackers. No ads. One cookie, only if you sign in.
Everything it knows comes from you, or your Circle.
Every fact has a source.
Every action has your hand on it.
Anything it knows, you can take back.
